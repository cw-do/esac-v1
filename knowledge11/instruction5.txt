Work hours are from 8am to 6pm during weekdays.

The following section describes what to do if a sample that has already seen the beam is broken.
If a sample that has already seen the beam is broken call the the Radiation Control Technicians (RCTs).
Do not touch the samples, do not try to clean them up.
