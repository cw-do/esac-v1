This document provides a general checklist for the Local Contact for the start of a neutron scattering experiment.

Is the IPTS number changed?
Are the samples checked in and have an ITEMS number assigned?
Is the SMA signed?
Is the ESS signed?
Are the users trained on the instrument?
Is there a box setup for irradiated samples?

Is the phyisical and the selected sample environment the same?
Are the sample positions calibrated?
Is the pinhole fully inserted?
Is the beamstop adjusted at each Q setting?
Is the sample table fast release fixed?
Is the airflow set to desired on or off state?
Is there container, empty cell and porous silica in the rack?
Is the neutron camera removed?
